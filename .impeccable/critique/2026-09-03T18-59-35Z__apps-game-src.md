## Design Specificity Verdict

WildGent feels strongly authored rather than category-interchangeable. Its mineral parchment,
forest green, cyan signal, ember human action, story typography, and shared-field language make a
coherent world. The main missed opportunity is that the landing's orbital specimen sells an
abstract signal interface rather than creatures, wilderness, and cooperation.

The deterministic scan reported two findings in `apps/game/src/styles.css`: a side-tab warning at
line 328 and a decorative-grid advisory at line 92. Both are false positives. The first is the
small triangular saved-state marker; the second is a restrained radial-dot atmospheric texture.
Neither should be changed for detector compliance.

No reliable browser overlay was available because the browser rejected mutable JavaScript URL
injection. Visual evidence came from fresh hosted landing and gameplay tabs; hosted evidence may
not exactly match the local checkout.

## Overall Impression

The game has a real point of view and a legible objective-to-action feedback loop. Its largest
opportunity is not more content: make the creatures and world emotionally tangible, then remove
interface that competes with them.

## What's Working

- The full-bleed world, editorial landing composition, and cyan/ember/gold vocabulary are distinct.
- Objective, contextual action, narrative feedback, and next objective form a clear play loop.
- The world remains visible and authoritative instead of becoming a conventional map dashboard.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The gameplay HUD competes with the world

At 1280x720, party cards, skill cards, Echo capabilities, the action prompt, and landmark rail take
substantial space. Collapse skills and capabilities into one compact instrument, show active and
next-relevant information by default, and recede secondary HUD while moving.

Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`.

### [P1] The landing sells an abstract signal instead of the game

The CSS orbit specimen shows neither a WildGent nor the reclaimed wilderness. Replace or augment
it with one authored frame from the actual low-poly world showing a creature and human/Echo impact.

Suggested command: `$impeccable bolder`.

### [P1] Three landing actions compete

Begin, Judge Demo, and Continue appear together when a save exists. Keep one contextual primary:
Continue for a save, Begin otherwise. Present Judge Demo as a clearly secondary, judge-specific
90-second rehearsal.

Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.

### [P2] Echo setup becomes a technical manual

The guide exposes browser mentions, model names, Chrome flags, tool IDs, origin trials, and a long
demo sequence. Keep one short player-facing Invite Echo instruction; move technical setup behind a
judge-only disclosure or external guide.

Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`.

### [P2] Important copy becomes tiny or disappears

Several party and capability labels are 8px, pause is glyph-only, and narrow layouts hide objective
and Echo context. Raise the readable floor and preserve semantic labels on narrow screens.

Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan, first-timer:** Cannot confidently choose among Begin, Judge Demo, and Continue; then
  meets many simultaneous HUD choices before learning which ones matter.
- **Alex, judge/power user:** The Judge Demo CTA does not explain its purpose, while the true path
  depends on external setup and a long scripted sequence.
- **Riley, low-vision keyboard player:** Tiny capability text and hidden narrow-screen context make
  an already spatial canvas harder to follow.

## Minor Observations

- Rename `Landing` to an in-world action such as `Return to camp`.
- `No account. No back-end.` reassures judges but weakens the opening fiction.
- Initials in circles identify creatures but do little to build creature attachment.
- The guardian battle panel is coherent but dense at 1280x720.

## Questions to Consider

- What information can disappear while the player moves without harming play?
- Can Echo's value be experienced in the first 30 seconds without technical setup?
- Should Judge Demo be a judge affordance rather than a peer of Begin Journey?
